Family & Financial Wellness

Financial wellness is a family journey. Whether you’re raising money-smart kids, preparing for college, or organizing your household finances, these learning blocks help your family build confidence at every stage.

A Family-Centered Approach to Financial Learning

Research from the CFPB, FDIC, and Federal Reserve shows that families who talk openly about money develop stronger financial habits, higher confidence, and greater long-term stability. This hub brings together three essential stages of financial wellness — helping families grow stronger together.

Kids & Teens

Children build lifelong habits early. Financial lessons between ages 6–18 shape how young people manage money, delay gratification, build savings, and understand needs vs. wants. Research shows early financial exposure leads to stronger saving behavior in adulthood.

Preparing for College

College planning blends financial readiness with realistic goal-setting. From understanding cost breakdowns to planning admissions timelines, families who plan early reduce borrowing, stress, and out-of-pocket expenses.

Learn More →

Family Financial Planning

Healthy household finances rely on alignment, clarity, and long-term planning. From budgeting as a family to organizing protection strategies and future goals, strong planning habits reduce stress and strengthen financial security.

Learn More →

Why Family Financial Wellness Matters

Studies from the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau show that families who practice shared financial habits are more resilient during unexpected events and more prepared for long-term goals like retirement, college, and homeownership.

  • Builds shared financial communication
  • Reduces financial stress for the entire household
  • Strengthens long-term preparedness and decision-making
  • Helps children build confidence with real-world skills
