Workshops & Webinars

Empowering families begins with education. At V & V Advisors, our workshops and webinars translate complex financial topics into practical, easy-to-understand lessons—supported by trusted, evidence-backed resources from the CFPB, FDIC, SEC, and other consumer protection organizations.

What You Will Learn

Our educational programs focus on essential, life-changing money skills every household should understand. Each session includes worksheets, tools, and real-life examples for stronger family decision-making.

  • How to build a resilient budget (FDIC Money Smart principles)
  • How insurance protects income, health, and family security (NAIC guidance)
  • How to manage debt strategically and avoid high-cost traps
  • How compounding, saving, and long-term investing work (SEC Investor Education)
  • How to plan for emergencies and long-term protection

Evidence Sources: FDIC Money Smart, CFPB, SEC Investor.gov
