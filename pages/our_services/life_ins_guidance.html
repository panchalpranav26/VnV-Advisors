What Is Personalized Protection?

Protection planning is the process of identifying the financial risks your family may face — and intentionally building safeguards around income, health, and long-term goals.

  • Protects income — your most valuable asset
  • Shields family from unexpected financial shocks
  • Ensures debt, housing, and daily expenses are covered
  • Supports future goals and generational wealth

Our guidance aligns with research and regulatory insights from the NAIC, CFPB, and California Department of Insurance.

The Four Pillars of a Strong Protection Strategy

A complete protection plan includes both insurance and non-insurance safeguards, giving your family layered, long-term security.

Pillar Purpose Examples
Income Protection Preserve earning power if you pass away or can’t work. Term life, disability insurance
Living Benefits Access funds during serious illness or injury. Chronic, critical, and terminal illness riders
Long-Term Stability Build savings and support legacy planning. Whole life, IUL, trust planning
Emergency & Legal Preparedness Protect daily life and major financial decisions. Emergency funds, will, POA, medical directives

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need more than one type of protection?

Most families require a combination of term life, disability protection, and emergency savings. Each tool serves a different purpose.

How often should I review my protection plan?

We recommend an annual review or sooner after major life events: marriage, a new child, home purchase, or job change.

Is personalized guidance expensive?

At V & V Advisors, educational guidance is always free. Our mission is to empower families, not sell products.
