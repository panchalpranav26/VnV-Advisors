Family Protection Planning Path

Your family’s needs evolve through life’s milestones. This step-by-step protection path helps you strengthen financial security—whether you’re just starting out or building legacy wealth.

How This Path Works

Families often ask “What should we do first?” or “Where are we in the protection journey?” This guide breaks everything into 7 clear stages, each aligned with major life events.

Every stage includes recommended protections, financial priorities, and evidence-backed reasons from regulatory and consumer agencies such as NAIC, CFPB, and the FDIC.

Your Life-Stage Protection Path

Focus: Build the foundation for financial resilience.

  • Create a beginner emergency fund (1–2 months)
  • Starter term life policy if supporting parents / shared rent
  • Consider disability insurance (income protection)

Sources: CFPB (cfpb.gov), NAIC.

Focus: Protect each other financially.

  • Joint income protection through term life insurance
  • Combine emergency funds → target 3 months
  • Review beneficiaries on all accounts
  • Basic estate privileges via will + POA

Source: NAIC Life Insurance Basics.

Focus: Long-term protection and stability.

  • Increase life insurance to cover income replacement, childcare, education
  • Consider permanent policies (WL/IUL)
  • Add child rider or children's whole life
  • Expand emergency fund to 3–6 months

Sources: CFPB Family Financial Education Resources.

Focus: Protect your largest asset.

  • Ensure mortgage protection via term life
  • Review homeowners + liability coverage
  • Consider umbrella liability policy

Sources: NAIC Homeowners Guide.

Focus: Build long-term wealth and optimize taxes.

  • IUL/VUL for supplemental tax-advantaged growth
  • Optimize retirement savings (401k, Roth IRA)
  • Review income replacement levels regularly

Sources: IRS Retirement Guidance; NAIC Permanent Policy Guide.

Focus: Multi-layer financial protection.

  • College funding strategy review
  • Explore LTC or hybrid life+LTC
  • Ensure your own income protection
  • Update estate documents and beneficiaries

Sources: CFPB College Resources, NAIC LTC Insurance Guide.

Focus: Lock in income and organize legacy.

  • Evaluate annuities for stable retirement income
  • Complete will, trust, POA, medical directives
  • Review legacy gifts or cash value policies
  • Final beneficiary audit

Sources: NAIC Annuity Buyer’s Guide; CFPB Estate Tools.
