Simplified & Guaranteed Life Insurance

Simplified and guaranteed life insurance options are designed for families who want protection without medical exams, complex underwriting, or long approval processes. These solutions help ensure everyone — regardless of age or health — can secure meaningful financial protection.

What Are Simplified & Guaranteed Life Insurance Products?

These products provide accessible, consumer-friendly coverage with little to no medical requirements. They are often used for:

  • Older adults needing final expense protection
  • Individuals with health challenges
  • Parents or grandparents securing early coverage for children
  • Families wanting small, stable, predictable policies

NAIC identifies these solutions as “limited underwriting products,” designed to improve access for consumers who may not qualify for traditional coverage.

Source: NAIC – Life Insurance Consumer Information

Guaranteed Issue Life Insurance

Guaranteed Issue Life Insurance requires no medical exam, no health questions, and no underwriting. It is designed for people who cannot qualify for other forms of life insurance due to age or health.

  • Guaranteed approval (typically ages 50–80)
  • Smaller coverage amounts ($5,000–$25,000)
  • Graded death benefit for first 2–3 years
  • Permanent coverage with fixed premiums

Source: III – Guaranteed Issue Basics

Final Expense Life Insurance

Final Expense insurance — also called burial or funeral insurance — is a form of simplified issue whole life insurance intended to cover end-of-life costs such as:

  • Funeral and burial services
  • Cremation costs
  • Outstanding medical bills
  • Small debts or family expenses

Policies typically offer $5,000–$40,000 in coverage, making them affordable and accessible for seniors.

Source: CFPB – Understanding End-of-Life Financial Choices

Children’s Whole Life Insurance

Children’s Whole Life Insurance provides lifelong coverage starting at a young age, often with extremely low premiums and guaranteed future insurability.

  • Guaranteed coverage regardless of future health
  • Cash value grows over time
  • Option to purchase additional coverage as an adult
  • Popular as a generational gift or financial foundation

Source: NAIC – Children’s Life Insurance Guidance

Questions to Ask Before Choosing

  • Do I want quick approval with minimal requirements?
  • Is this for final expenses, legacy planning, or early lifetime coverage?
  • Will traditional underwriting offer higher value or larger coverage?
  • Can my budget support permanent coverage with fixed premiums?
  • Do I understand graded death benefits and how they work?
