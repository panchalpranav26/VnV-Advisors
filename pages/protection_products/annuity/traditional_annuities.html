Traditional Annuities

Traditional annuities offer predictable growth and guaranteed income — providing families with stability, safety, and clarity as they plan for retirement. These solutions are backed by insurer guarantees and designed for low-risk savers seeking dependable results.

What Are Traditional Annuities?

Traditional annuities are conservative insurance products that offer guaranteed interest, stable growth, and predictable income. They are regulated savings and income tools backed by state guaranty associations and insurer reserves.

  • Protected principal — no market-loss exposure
  • Fixed, contractually guaranteed interest
  • Tax-deferred growth
  • Option for lifetime income

According to the Insurance Information Institute (III), annuities play a critical role in ensuring retirement income that cannot be outlived — especially as life expectancy increases.

Source: III – Annuities Overview

What Makes an Annuity “Traditional”?

The term “traditional” refers to annuities with fixed guarantees and no market volatility. They prioritize safety, stability, and predictable income.

  • No market-linked performance (unlike FIAs or VAs)
  • Fixed rates set by the insurer
  • Clear contract terms with minimal complexity
  • Ideal for risk-averse savers

Fixed Annuities

Fixed annuities provide stable, guaranteed interest rates, similar to a long-term CD — but with tax-deferred growth and higher yield potential.

  • Guaranteed, contract-locked interest
  • Principal protection
  • Tax-deferred accumulation
  • Often higher rates than bank CDs

NAIC guidelines emphasize fixed annuities as low-risk income planning tools for retirees and conservative savers.

Source: NAIC – Understanding Annuities

Immediate Annuities

Immediate annuities convert a lump sum into guaranteed income that begins within 6–12 months. This can be structured for life or a set number of years.

  • Predictable monthly income
  • Option for lifetime payments
  • Protection against outliving retirement savings (longevity risk)
  • Simple, contractually guaranteed payouts

Source: III – Immediate Annuities

Multi-Year Guaranteed Annuities (MYGA)

MYGAs offer a guaranteed interest rate for a set number of years, often 3, 5, 7, or 10. They are a tax-efficient alternative to CDs with predictable, contract-backed growth.

  • Guaranteed rate for a specific term
  • Principal protection with no market exposure
  • Tax-deferred interest accumulation
  • Often higher yields than fixed annuities with shorter terms

Source: CFPB – Annuities & Retirement Planning

Who Are Traditional Annuities Best For?

  • Individuals seeking low-risk, predictable growth
  • Retirees needing guaranteed income
  • Families looking for CD alternatives with tax benefits
  • People preparing for future income gaps
  • Conservative investors who prefer safety over performance
