Specialized Annuities

Specialized annuities provide long-term income stability and financial structure for families who need predictable income, legal settlement protection, or guaranteed future payouts. These solutions are often used in retirement, legal settlements, and strategic income planning.

What Are Specialized Annuities?

Specialized annuities include products designed for guaranteed, structured, or legally mandated income scenarios. Unlike growth-focused annuities, these solutions prioritize predictability, protection, and long-term stability.

  • Income Annuities: Guaranteed lifetime or period-certain income
  • Deferred Income Annuities: Higher future income for individuals planning far ahead
  • Structured Settlement Annuities: Court-approved, tax-advantaged payouts for legal cases

The NAIC and CFPB note that income assurances are a foundational retirement tool, particularly for households concerned with outliving savings (longevity risk).

Sources: NAIC – Annuity Buyer’s Guide; CFPB – Retirement Income Planning

Income & Immediate Annuities

Income annuities provide a simple promise: turn a lump sum into a predictable monthly income stream for life or a set number of years. Payments typically begin within 12 months.

  • Lifetime income options (cannot outlive payments)
  • Fixed period options (10, 15, 20+ years)
  • Highest income per premium among annuity types
  • Ideal for retirees transitioning from work income

The Insurance Information Institute (III) highlights immediate annuities as among the most reliable tools for longevity protection.

Source: III – Income Annuities Overview

Deferred Income Annuities (DIA)

Deferred Income Annuities offer significantly higher future income because payments start many years after purchase. Families use DIAs to create a guaranteed “retirement paycheck” later in life.

  • Often purchased in your 40s, 50s, or early 60s
  • Provides guaranteed future income regardless of market performance
  • Helps manage longevity and late-retirement income gaps
  • Lower cost than immediate annuities for same future payout

According to the SEC, DIAs help retirees avoid the risk of depleting savings in advanced age.

Source: SEC – Retirement Income Planning Guide

Structured Settlement Annuities

Structured settlement annuities provide tax-advantaged, long-term payments to individuals receiving compensation from personal injury or other legal cases. These annuities are typically arranged through courts and offer decades of guaranteed income.

  • Customized payment schedules
  • Often tax-free for qualified cases
  • Designed to ensure long-term support for beneficiaries
  • Protected under federal and state structured settlement regulations

The National Structured Settlements Trade Association (NSSTA) emphasizes these annuities as a key consumer protection mechanism ensuring funds last as long as intended.

Source: NSSTA – Structured Settlement Education

Who Are Specialized Annuities Best For?

  • Families needing guaranteed, predictable income
  • Individuals planning for future retirement income gaps
  • People receiving legal settlements and needing structured support
  • Anyone prioritizing safety and guaranteed cash flow
  • Investors who prefer protection over market fluctuation
