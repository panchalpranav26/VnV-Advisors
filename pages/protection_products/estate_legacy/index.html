  • Living Trust (optional but valuable)
  • Financial Power of Attorney
  • Medical / Healthcare Directive

Your Family Legacy Path

Building a legacy doesn’t happen at once — it happens through intentional steps. This simplified path helps families structure their legal and emotional preparations.

Step 1 — Organize Your Important Documents

Identify and secure your critical personal, legal, insurance, and financial files.

Step 2 — Assign Trusted Decision Makers

Choose who will handle finances, medical care, and guardianship if needed.

Step 3 — Define Your Wishes & Preferences

Clarify your medical care choices, family legacy preferences, and end-of-life planning.

Step 4 — Coordinate Beneficiaries

Life insurance, retirement accounts, and financial accounts must be aligned with your estate wishes.

Step 5 — Review Every 12–24 Months

Life changes — marriage, children, home purchase. Your documents should change with you.
