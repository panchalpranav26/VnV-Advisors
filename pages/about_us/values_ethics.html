Our Values & Code of Ethics

At V & V Advisors, everything begins with our commitment to education, transparency, and family-first guidance. These values ensure that every conversation, tool, and recommendation genuinely supports the long-term financial well-being of the families we serve.

Our Core Values

These core values shape how we teach, guide, and support families through financial literacy, protection planning, and long-term stability.

Integrity

We operate with honesty, fairness, and full transparency. No pressure. No hidden agendas.

Aligned with ethical guidelines from NAIC and California DOI.

Education First

Our priority is teaching families how money works — not selling products.

Based on frameworks from CFPB, FDIC Money Smart.

Trust

Trust is earned through clarity, reliability, and consistent guidance.

We avoid jargon, pushy sales, and fear-driven messaging.

Empowerment

We help families gain the confidence to make informed financial decisions.

Tools and coaching are built for real families — not academics.

Respect & Compassion

Every family’s story is unique. We honor cultural, financial, and personal backgrounds.

Our guidance is judgment-free and human-centered.

Our Code of Ethics

Our ethical standards guide every conversation, resource, and recommendation. This visual journey reflects the path we walk with families — grounded in trust, transparency, and responsible education.

Client-First Commitment

We always act in the best interest of the family — never a product or commission. Your goals guide every recommendation.

Clear & Honest Communication

Every concept is explained in plain English — no jargon, no pressure, and no misleading claims.

Strict Confidentiality

We protect your personal and financial information with secure processes and ethical handling.

Transparency in Compensation

Any referral partnerships, commissions, or compensation are disclosed clearly and upfront.

Continuous Learning

We stay updated with consumer-protection laws and ethical standards from NAIC, SEC, CFPB, FDIC, and the California Department of Insurance.

No Fear-Based Selling

No scare tactics. No urgency tricks. We guide with clarity — not intimidation or pressure.

Regulatory Compliance

All guidance follows strict regulatory standards from:

  • California Department of Insurance (CDI)
  • National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC)
  • SEC Investor Education

Built on Trust. Guided by Education. Focused on You.

These values and ethical commitments shape the way we help families build lasting financial strength — with confidence, clarity, and peace of mind.
